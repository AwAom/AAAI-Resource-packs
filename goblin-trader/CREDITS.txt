AAAI Goblin Trader pack credits

The goblin head models and textures (assets/aaai/models/item/*goblin_trader_head.json, assets/aaai/textures/item/*goblin_trader_head.png)
are ORIGINAL AAAI art (MIT, see the repository LICENSE), generated by tools/goblin_head.py. The item definitions of the base blocks
(assets/minecraft/items/moss_block.json, nether_wart_block.json) only select our model by custom_model_data and fall back to the vanilla block model; no Mojang pixels or colours.

The idea and the trade table of the goblin traders come from "Goblin Traders" by MrCrayfish (MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2023 MrCrayfish, https://github.com/MrCrayfish/GoblinTraders). No texture, model or sound of that mod is used here.

The pack only changes moss / nether wart blocks that carry custom_model_data "goblin_trader_head" or "vein_goblin_trader_head" (worn by
our goblin traders); every other block item and every wandering trader looks vanilla.

pack.png (icon): original composition by AwAom (goblin head model of this pack, trade-window frame and all item icons hand-drawn in pixel art). No Mojang pixels, no third-party art.
