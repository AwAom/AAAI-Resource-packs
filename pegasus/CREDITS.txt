AAAI Pegasus pack credits

The horse coat texture (assets/aaaimob/textures/entity/equipment/horse_body/pegasus_coat.png) is based on "Pegasus"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pegasus-5b1018a2e0844747bae1ca8933820619) by DibArts (https://sketchfab.com/youssefe22)
licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes made: the model textures were recoloured (pearl coat, aurora mane and tail, gold hooves) and mapped onto the
vanilla horse texture layout. Only the layout (alpha mask) of the vanilla horse texture was used, no Mojang pixels.

The Pegasus saddle texture (assets/aaaimob/textures/entity/equipment/horse_saddle/pegasus_saddle.png) is original AAAI art
(MIT, see the repository LICENSE); only the layout of the vanilla saddle texture was used, no Mojang pixels.
