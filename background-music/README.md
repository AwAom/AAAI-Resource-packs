# aaai-background-music

Optional music pack of the AAAI Minecraft server. Players without it play normally, they just hear no music.

| Sound event | Where it plays | Track |
|---|---|---|
| `aaai:music.throne_boss` | the throne boss fight of AAAI-Original-Dungeon (starts when the guards wake, loops every 4 minutes) | "Battle of the Ages" by NoCopyrightSound633 |

- Java: `aaai-background-music-<ver>.zip` (resource pack, add it as a server resource pack or in the `resourcepacks` folder). Bedrock: `aaai-background-music-bedrock-<ver>.mcpack` (Geyser `packs/` folder).
- The track is stereo Ogg Vorbis (128 kbps), +4 dB with a limiter (about -11.9 LUFS) and a 2 s / 4 s fade, tuned by ear against the vanilla Pigstep disc.
- More tracks will be added as new sound events in the same pack.
